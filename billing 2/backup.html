Questions

Before you ask.

Anything else — bring it to the demo call, or write to us at hello@grovixo.com.

Will it work with my existing thermal printer?

Almost certainly. Grovixo speaks raw ESC/POS — the standard nearly every receipt printer understands — over USB, Bluetooth (BLE) or your network (port 9100). 58mm and 80mm papers are presets, and any other width can be set exactly. You can test-pair your printer from Settings before ever relying on it at the counter.

Is it really GST-compliant?

Yes. Every product carries an HSN code and GST slab, invoices show the CGST/SGST or IGST split based on your state, and the GST report gives you an invoice-level tax breakdown you can export to CSV and hand straight to your CA for filing.

Can my staff use it without seeing my profits?

Yes — that's exactly what roles are for. Create a cashier role that can create invoices and manage customers but has no access to reports, settings or user management. Permissions are enforced on the server, and every user's logins and actions are logged.

What happens to my data? Can I take a backup?

Your data stays yours. From Settings → Data & Backups you can create a full database backup in one click, download it, and restore it later. Destructive actions sit in a separate, clearly marked danger zone and always require confirmation.

I sell in different units — boxes, pieces, kilograms. Handled?

Handled. Every product can have a primary and secondary unit with a conversion factor (e.g. 1 Box = 12 Pieces). Buy in one unit, sell in another — stock levels and valuations stay correct automatically.

Do I need special hardware or a fast computer?

No. Grovixo runs in the browser on the computer, tablet or phone you already own. A basic USB barcode scanner and any ESC/POS thermal printer complete the counter — both optional to start.
